We'll work together over video chat.

Before your first session, we'll meet for thirty minutes. We'll talk about your project, understand what goals you're working towards and get them right-sized so you know what you can achieve in a week.

We'll meet for an hour. I'll remind you of your goal, and we'll work on it together, learning and solving problems.

Then it's your time until we meet again, with a twist: we'll agree on a way for you to keep in touch in between sessions, a way that works for you. Bio

I'm Gwendolyn James. I've been working professionally with linux and infrastructure since 2004, from small startups self-hosting on hand-built systems to Fortune 500 companies such as Oracle and Workday, from Megabytes to Petabytes. I was a self-taught Principal Engineer at a rocket engine factory, where I built and designed production and its deployments as a solo infrastructure engineer. I didn't want to be paged nights and weekends: it was two years before we had our first infrastructure-related issue.

Through all this, my passion has been helping my peers grow into excellent, self-disciplined engineers.

I work in classic data centre infrastructure, I work in cloud infrastructure; I work on build systems and write build jobs; I work in Kubernetes and with Terraform; I write shell scripts, go programs, python.

Michael, who I worked with, was newly Staff at a company without a real agreement about what staff meant. His lead couldn't say what was supposed to be different now. We met every two weeks. I helped him learn Staff Engineer level discipline and habits, and also showed him new tools along the way.

Gwyn, you have changed the way that I work and what I know I can achieve. — Michael R., Staff Engineer
